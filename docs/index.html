FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions

Is ProAssist AI completely free? ▼
Yes! ProAssist AI is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. You can use your own free Google Gemini API key (which includes generous free tier requests from Google AI Studio).
Where do I get a free Google Gemini API Key? ▼
Visit Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com), sign in with your Google account, and click "Create API key". Paste it into ProAssist AI's Settings dialog.
Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen). Is it safe? ▼
Yes. Microsoft SmartScreen displays this alert for all newly built independent or open-source executables that have not purchased expensive corporate code-signing certificates. Simply click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
What are the system requirements? ▼
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), 4 GB of RAM, a working microphone and speaker or headset, and an active internet connection for Gemini Live streaming.
